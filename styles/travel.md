# Style: travel (faceless)

> Learned on 2026-11-27 from 2 reference reels in `references/travel/`, both by Wen Hui. Two more reels couldn't be downloaded because Instagram wanted a login.
> With only one creator studied, this style is a starting point. Add 2-3 more travel references later to round it out.
> The theme: **faceless travel.** You never talk to camera. POV and walking shots carry the video, with a clear payoff or reveal.
> Claude adds your feedback to **Do / Don't** after each edit, so the style improves over time.

## What this style is for
- Type of content: two formats.
  1. **Quick moment** (10-15s): a joke or setup line, then a reveal (ref: Wen Hui DbDiyQxstk2).
  2. **Place guide** (45-60s): a highlight hook, a walk-through, practical info and a verdict (ref: Wen Hui DgDD0zgsxjh).
- Who is on screen: nobody facing the camera. Show your feet, hands, back or side only (ref: both). Audio is music only (quick moment), or a voiceover recorded separately over a music bed (place guide).

## Format
- Aspect ratio / resolution: 9:16, 1080x1920
- Frame rate: 30 fps (ref: both)
- Typical length: 10-15s for a quick moment, 45-60s for a place guide

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
- **Quick moment**: a POV shot (e.g. looking down at your feet walking) with a 2-line setup in meme format: line 1 in quotes, line 2 starting with a colon, e.g. `"I need a morning walk"` / `: the walk` (ref: DbDiyQxstk2 @ 0:00)
- **Place guide**: a montage of the 4 best shots at about 0.6s each, under an animated title with the place name (ref: DgDD0zgsxjh @ 0:00-0:02.5)
- Cuts in the first 3 seconds: 0 for a quick moment (one continuous walking shot), 4 for a place guide

## Pacing
- Average shot length: about 2s for a place guide (ref: DgDD0zgsxjh, 29 cuts in 59s). One long shot of up to 8s, then quick payoff shots, for a quick moment (ref: DbDiyQxstk2).
- Pauses kept between sentences (voiceover): short, about 0.3s
- Cut timing: **on voiceover phrases** for a place guide, so each new sentence gets a new shot. Cut at the reveal for a quick moment. Neither reference cuts on the music beat.
- Speed changes: none. Smooth, real-speed walking.

## Captions and on-screen text
- **Voiceover captions** (place guide): one short phrase at a time (3-6 words), white medium-weight sans, thin shadow, no box, centered at about 84% from the top, 1.5-4s each (ref: DgDD0zgsxjh). The reference text is very small (about 27px). Use about 40px so it's readable on a phone.
- **Setup text** (quick moment): thin white sans, no outline, 2 lines centered at about 40% from the top. Remove it right as the reveal shot starts (ref: DbDiyQxstk2 @ 0:10.2). Use about 36px for readability (the reference is about 24px).
- **Title card** (place guide): the place name in a white rounded handwritten-style font, about 100px, thick dark outline plus drop shadow, with a few small hand-drawn sparkles or an underline swoosh. It pops or writes on over 0.4s, centered at about 47%. Add a smaller subtitle under it in a soft accent color (mint green), about 55px (ref: DgDD0zgsxjh @ 0:00).
- Info such as prices and opening hours goes into the voiceover captions at the moment they're said (ref: DgDD0zgsxjh, ticket price)
- All caps?: no
- Emojis: none apart from the title doodles

## Footage and framing
- Main shots: handheld or gimbal walking shots, POV at foot level, hands holding things (tickets, food, souvenirs), and low angles looking up at buildings or landmarks (ref: both)
- You, if shown: from behind or the side, posing or walking away, never talking to camera (ref: DgDD0zgsxjh @ 0:42)
- Reveal shots: a view through a window, the wide landmark or the destination (ref: DbDiyQxstk2 @ 0:10.2, a plane wing over clouds)
- Zooms / punch-ins: none. Movement comes from walking with the camera.

## Graphics
- Titles: only the place-guide title card above
- Tool to use: HyperFrames for the animated title card, Remotion for the voiceover captions

## Transitions
- Hard cuts only (ref: both)

## Color
- Bright and vivid daylight with punchy blue skies, slightly warm, higher saturation (+10-15%) (ref: DgDD0zgsxjh). Keep the quick-moment format natural (ref: DbDiyQxstk2).

## Music & sound effects
- Music type and energy: a light, feel-good song at 110-120 BPM (ref: DbDiyQxstk2)
- Music level: the main audio for a quick moment. Keep it about 7 dB under the voiceover for a place guide (ref: DgDD0zgsxjh).
- Sound effects: none
- Final loudness: -14 LUFS (both references measured -14.0 to -14.1)

## Ending
- **Quick moment**: end on the reveal shot (ref: DbDiyQxstk2)
- **Place guide**: bring back a shot from the hook, then end with a one-line verdict in the captions, such as "worth the trip" or "skip it" (ref: DgDD0zgsxjh @ 0:52). Don't use "follow for more".

## Do / Don't
*Your corrections, saved as rules. Never deleted, only added to or refined.*

**Do**
- Plan the payoff shot before filming, so the setup has something to reveal
- Keep your face out of frame

**Don't**
- Don't copy the reference creator's exact jokes or title design. Use the techniques with your own words and look.
